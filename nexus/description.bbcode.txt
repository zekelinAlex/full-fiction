[size=5][b]Description[/b][/size]
Split Fiction always splits the screen in half, even in online co-op where each of you sits at your own monitor. That's half your screen spent on a view your partner already sees on theirs.

[b]Full Fiction[/b] gives you your whole screen back. Press [b]F10[/b] and your character fills the entire monitor. Press it again to peek at your partner's view, or once more to go back to the classic split screen.

The mod uses the game's own built-in fullscreen mechanism (the same one Hazelight uses in certain story moments) through UE4SS, instead of hacking the renderer. That's why everything still looks and behaves like the original game.

[size=5][b]Installation instructions[/b][/size]
Two ways to install, both on the Files tab. Pick whichever you prefer, the mod is the same.

[b]Option A: manual install (no exe)[/b]
[list=1]
[*]Close the game and download [b]Full Fiction (manual install)[/b].
[*]In Steam: right-click Split Fiction > Manage > Browse local files, then go one folder up to [i]steamapps\common[/i].
[*]Drag the [b]Split Fiction[/b] folder from the zip into [i]common[/i] and confirm merging the folders.
[*]Launch the game and press [b]F10[/b].
[/list]
Auto mode: open [i]Split\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss\Mods\FullFiction\Scripts\fullfiction_config.lua[/i] in Notepad and change false to true.
To uninstall, delete [i]Split\Binaries\Win64\dwmapi.dll[/i] and the [i]ue4ss[/i] folder next to it.

[b]Option B: installer[/b]
[list=1]
[*]Close the game.
[*]Download and run [b]FullFiction-Setup.exe[/b].
[*]The installer finds your Steam copy of Split Fiction automatically. If it doesn't, click "Choose game folder..." and point it to your Split Fiction folder.
[*](Optional) tick [b]Auto mode[/b].
[*]Click [b]Install[/b] and launch the game from Steam as usual.
[/list]
Windows SmartScreen may warn you because the installer isn't signed with a paid certificate. Click "More info" > "Run anyway". Some antivirus tools also flag [i]dwmapi.dll[/i]: that's a false positive, it's how UE4SS (the mod loader) hooks into Unreal Engine games.

[b]Uninstall:[/b] run the installer again and click [b]Uninstall[/b]. The mod never modifies game files, so Steam's "Verify integrity of game files" also brings everything back to vanilla.

[size=5][b]Main features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]F10 view cycle:[/b] split screen > your view fullscreen > partner's view fullscreen > split screen
[*][b]Cutscenes stay intact.[/b] When a cutscene starts the mod steps aside and the game shows it exactly as intended.
[*][b]Smart about special moments.[/b] When the game switches to its own special screen layouts or forces a fullscreen view, the mod waits instead of fighting it.
[*][b]Optional auto mode.[/b] After every cutscene and loading screen your view goes fullscreen automatically, no key press needed. A bit less predictable in rare spots, but the most seamless experience. Toggle it in the installer anytime.
[*][b]Client-side only.[/b] Nothing about networking changes. Your partner doesn't need the mod and keeps playing normally.
[*][b]Works with Friend's Pass[/b] on either side.
[*][b]No performance cost.[/b] Rendering one view instead of two is, if anything, lighter on your GPU.
[/list]

[b]Known limitations[/b]
[list]
[*]Meant for [b]online[/b] co-op. In local co-op on one screen it would hide player 2's view, so keep it on split screen there.
[*]Game updates may break the mod until it's updated. If F10 stops working after a patch, check back here.
[*]Split Fiction occasionally crashes with a "Fatal error" even without mods. If you get crashes noticeably more often with the mod, please report it and attach [i]Split\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss\UE4SS.log[/i].
[/list]

[size=5][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Split Fiction, [b]Steam version[/b] (the EA app version is not supported yet)
[*]Windows 10 / 11 (the installer uses .NET Framework 4.8, which ships with Windows)
[*]UE4SS is bundled, no separate download needed
[/list]
